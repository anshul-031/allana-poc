=== PAGE 2 ===

User interface  
 
1. Authorization  
For the first entry to the portal, the client will receive a welcome letter indicating the 
login (e-mail) and password to enter. After the first authorization, the client immediately goes 
to the password change page, where he must enter and confirm the new password. After 
changing the password, the client needs to re -authorize with the login (e -mail) and a new 
password. 
 
On the authorization page, users have the option to reset their passwords. The 
password can be reset by using the email used for authorization. 
 
2. Header menu 
After successful authorization, the client goes to the Dashboard page. The client sees 
menu items: 
- Dashboard - transfer to the dashboard page on click 
- Products Catalog - transfer to products catalog page on click 
- Customized Product - transfer to customized product page on click 
- Shipments List - transfer to shipments list page on click 
- Sales Plan - transfer to sales plan page on click 
- Reports - transfer to reports page on click 
- News - transfer to news page on click 


=== PAGE 3 ===

- Claims and Requests - transfer to request page after click 
 
 
3. Header 
3.1 Drop-down list with related companies 
 If the client has related companies, he can view them in the drop-down list with related 
companies (in the header on the left). The client can go to the desired company by simply 
clicking on it. After switching to another company, the client will see all the data of this 
company. 
 
 
 3.2 Notifications 
When the client clicks on the "bell", a context menu of messages for the client appears. 
Types of messages that may receive: 
- a message about changing the status of an application for a custom product 
- request registration and status change 
- notification about the arrival of the delivery (2 weeks before the arrival and at the time 
when the delivery has arrived) 
- license updates 
Available actions for the client: 
- mark a specific message as read 


=== PAGE 4 ===

- mark all messages as read 
- filtering of unread messages

 By clicking on the "settings menu", the user can go to the following pages:

- Licenses (if the user’s country is Saudi Arabia) 
- Action Log 
- Change the password 
- Logout

=== PAGE 7 ===

5. Members List 
On the “Members List” page are displayed all users belonging to the parent company. 
The customer can view the table of users. The following information is displayed in the user 
table: 
- EMAIL 
- PHONE 
- FIRST NAME 
- CREATED DATE 
 
  
 The customer can create an additional user for his company, for this he needs to click 
on the “Add User” button. 
 


=== PAGE 8 ===

On the page for creating a user, the customer needs to fill in the user data. And in the 
form on the right side, put check boxes to the appropriate settings and permissions, and click 
the “Save” button. 
 
 After that, the created user will appear in the members list. 
 The customer can also edit a previously created user. To do this, he needs to select 
a user from the table and click the "Edit" button. Next, you need to edit the required fields 
and click "Save" 
 
 


=== PAGE 9 ===

6. Action Log 
On this page, the customer can see the actions performed with users on the "Members 
List" page (i.e. adding, editing or deleting a user). The following information is displayed in the 
logs table: 
- DATE 
- AUTHOR 
- COMMENT 
 
 
 
7. Password change 
 The client can change the password by clicking on the context menu of his personal 
profile in the upper right corner and selecting the "Password" link in the drop-down list. 
 
 A modal window will open to change the password. The client needs to enter the 
current password, new password and confirm the new password.

 In the modal window that opens, confirm the exit by clicking the “Yes” button.

17. Claims and Requests 


=== PAGE 31 ===

To create a request for information on products, certificates and to create a claim, the 
user needs to select the Claims and Requests section.
 
On the main page of the section: 
1. It is possible to choose the required request form 
2. Find a previously created request\complaint by number or name 
3. Find the necessary request/complaint in the list using filtering 
 
All records on the main page have a date of creation, a number, information about the 
service and the object of the request, and a status that changes accordingly during the review 
of the request/complaint: 
 
View information about the selected record in a short format, possibly by clicking on 
the arrow next to the date. 


=== PAGE 32 ===

 
The company's response to the request (if available) is also displayed in a short form. 
The number of fields and content in the short form differs depending on the request. 
By clicking on the inscription "Details", a transition is made to the page of the selected 
record, where, according to the fields of the form, the information provided by the author (portal 
user\customer\client) is displayed. The ability to edit and make changes is not possible. 
Forms for requests/complaints differ among themselves both in terms of the number 
of fields and their obligation to fill them out. 
To start filling out the form, just click on the button with the form's name. For example 
"Claims Form" 
 
The block of information "Personal data" is the same in all forms. Information on the 
client is filled in automatically. All fields are editable except "Email". 
The next block in the Claims Form is General Information 


=== PAGE 33 ===

 
● If there is a "*" sign next to the field, it means that the field is mandatory, and if there 
is no information in it, the form will not be saved on the portal. 
● The field next to which there is no "*" mark can be filled in as desired. 
● When there is an arrow in the field for filling, it means that there is a list for selection in 
the field, and it is possible to fill it only by choosing one of the proposed values. 
Block Product description:
 
● On all forms where the "Product Name" field is present, it is filled in automatically after 
filling in the "Product Article" field. 
● In some fields there are hints on the data filling format, such as the date, where the 
system will set the separators, or the weight, where the separator is a dot, after which 
it is possible to specify 4 more characters. 
● The "Add Product" button allows you to add up to 10 products to 1 request form. 
Complaint Details block:
 


=== PAGE 34 ===

● Depending on the form, the "Description" field may be required or optional. Text field 
without punctuation and language restrictions. It is recommended to use up to 1000 
characters. 
● The "Attach" button allows you to add files. After saving the form, it will also be possible 
to view the files when opening the form by clicking "Details". 
● The "Additional contact" field is a field for adding an e -mail to which, if necessary, a 
copy of the response to the request should be sent. 
● "Send" button - when pressed, the form with filled fields is saved and automatically 
forwarded to the responsible employees for consideration. The form will not be saved 
if at least one of the required fields is not filled in.  
Info Request form 
The block of information "Personal data" is identical in all forms. 
In the form, it is possible to choose the direction of the application. 
 
The appearance and content will also change depending on the selected type of form. 
The principle of filling the fields and their algorithm remains similar to that described in the 
"Claims Form".
