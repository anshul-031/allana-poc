MHP has converted a relationship-driven export business into a digitally enabled 
customer ecosystem where product discovery, onboarding, shipment visibility, 
ﬁnancial transparency and service support are largely self-managed by the 
customer. 
For poultry and retail this is probably where the industry is heading over the next 5 
years. 
 
From the user manual and portal structure, MHP appears to have built a mature B2B 
customer self-service platform rather than a simple order booking system. 
 
The portal combines six important functions into one customer interface: 
1. Customer onboarding  
2. Product discovery  
3. Customized product development  
4. Shipment tracking  
5. Financial transparency  
6. Customer service and claims management  
This means the customer depends less on sales executives for routine activities.

3. Customized Product Requests 
Customers can submit requests for: 
 new cuts  
 new packaging  
 new recipes  
 new speciﬁcations  
and then track progress through statuses; In Review, Analysis, Accepted, Declined. 
This effectively digitizes interaction between: 
Customer → Sales → R&D → Production → Customer 
For example: Need 450g vacuum packed goat cubes for UAE retail. 
Instead of endless emails, the customer simply submits the request. This is extremely 
useful for RTC/RTE and retail businesses.

 container number

5. Financial Dashboard 
This is something meat exporters almost never provide. Customers can see: 
 credit limit  
 receivables  
 overdue receivables  
 available limit. 
For distributors this is extremely valuable. 
Example: 
 Credit Limit: $2 million 
 Outstanding: $1.2 million 
 Available: $800,000 
Customer immediately knows whether additional orders can be placed. 
 
6. Claims and Service 
Notiﬁcations include: 
 shipment arrival alerts  
 custom product status  
 claim updates  
 license expiry alerts.  
This converts the portal into a customer service desk rather than merely an ordering 
tool. 
 
Assessment 
This is not an ecommerce platform. 
It is closer to: 
SAP Customer Portal + CRM + Shipment Tracker + Product Catalog + Service Desk
